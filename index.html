QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

A few things you might wonder.

What is Fari?

Fari is an AI companion for everyday moments. You can journal your thoughts, practice mindfulness, and talk through your day with a friend who's always there.

Is Fari free?

Fari is free to start. You can begin talking with Fari and exploring daily features at no cost.

How does Fari remember things?

Fari remembers what you choose to share, so your conversations can build on each other over time rather than starting from zero each day.

Is Fari a therapist?

No. Fari is an AI companion, not a therapist or emergency service. If you need professional support, please reach out to a qualified mental health provider.

What can I talk to Fari about?

Anything you'd like — your day, your dreams, a small win, or just a passing thought. Fari is here to listen and be present.

How is Fari different from other AI companions?

Fari focuses on daily rituals — journaling, mindfulness, and language practice — rather than just chatting. With a unique angel companion character, voice and video interactions, and memory that builds over time, Fari is designed to be a gentle presence in your everyday life, not just another chatbot.

Can I use Fari as a daily journal?

Yes. Fari includes a daily journaling feature where you can write about your day, track your mood, and watch your story unfold over time. Your entries can also feed into Fari's memory, so your conversations can build on what you've written.

What languages does Fari support?

Fari is available in English, Chinese, Portuguese, Japanese, and Korean. You can practice any of these languages with Fari in a safe, judgment-free space, one sentence at a time.
